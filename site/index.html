Good to know

Quick answers.

What are your hours?

Monday through Friday 6am–5pm, Saturday 8am–2pm, and we’re closed on Sundays.

What’s in a Beauty tea?

Each Beauty Tea is 39 calories with 115mg of caffeine, 6g carbs, 2g protein, biotin & collagen, and 21 vitamins & minerals. Not sure which flavor to try? Ask at the counter and we’ll help you pick.

How much protein is in your shakes?

Every meal replacement shake has 24g of protein, with 200–250 calories and 6–15g of sugar depending on the flavor.

Do you have something for kids?

Yes! Our Kid’s Teas come in fun flavors like Aqua Man, Nemo and Wonder Woman, with 320mg of electrolytes and 21 vitamins & minerals.

Can I order ahead?

Give us a call at (615) 437-1008 and we’ll have it ready for in-store pickup.

Is the menu always the same?

Our core teas and shakes are always on, and we rotate seasonal flavors throughout the year. Follow us on Instagram for the latest.
